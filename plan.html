All inputs are in Ghana cedis (GHS). These are example amounts, not current travel prices.

Your estimate will appear here.

What does it include?

The calculator assumes one fewer night than the number of days. A one-day trip has no overnight stay.

Total = travellers × (nights × accommodation + days × food) + group transport.

Activities and entry fees are excluded. Change the inputs and calculate again to compare options.

Before you go

  • Confirm transport and accommodation.
  • Check destination access and opening times.
  • Budget separately for activities.
  • Keep an emergency allowance.
